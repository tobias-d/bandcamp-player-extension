#TitleBPMT

Play the album in Bandcamp's official player on the left. The panel on the right is the extension's UI with this album's real analysis — waveforms & BPM precomputed by the extension's pipeline. Pick a track in the panel and the Bandcamp player loads it; on Bandcamp, the panel does all of this live and follows playback by itself.

Side A · Features

A1Real BPM, not a guess

Tempo is analyzed from the actual audio with Essentia — the same signal-analysis library used in MIR research. No metadata scraping.

A2Key detection

An electronic-music-tuned key pipeline that's honest: one key, two candidates, or nothing when the evidence is weak.

A3Tempo Adjust

Preview any track at your set's tempo. Hear how it'll actually sit in the mix before you buy.

A4Waveform seeking

A real rendered waveform. Skip the intro, jump to the drop, check the breakdown.

A5Wishlist that keeps up

Heart albums and tracks from the panel — synced against Bandcamp's own collection data, so state is never stale.

Intermission

Stop losing your place.

Digging on Bandcamp means twenty open tabs and a player that dies on every page load. Bandcamp Deck keeps one persistent panel pinned to whatever's playing: browse on, queue the next find, check the BPM, heart it, move on. Your flow never breaks.

Side B · Engineering

How it's engineered

Most player extensions wrap the page's <audio> tag. Bandcamp Deck runs its own audio engine.

B1Real time-stretching, not playback-rate

Tempo Adjust runs SignalSmith Stretch inside an AudioWorklet on the real-time audio thread — tempo changes, pitch stays. The browser's playbackRate shortcut is never used, not even as a fallback: if the worklet can't run, the control disables itself instead of degrading silently.

B2Gapless, like a DJ deck

Two complete audio hosts run at all times. The next track is decoded and loaded on the silent one while the current track keeps playing, then the two swap — the expensive work never touches the audible path.

B3Instant seeking

The full track is decoded to PCM in memory, so dragging the waveform just moves a read position. No rebuffering, no spinner.

B4Analysis on your machine

BPM and key come from Essentia (WebAssembly) running on the decoded audio in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
