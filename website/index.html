The DJ-ready player for Bandcamp

Bandcamp // Deck adds a floating player with BPM and key analysis, tempo control, and a clickable waveform, working across Bandcamp pages like Feed, Collections, Wishlists, and Discover.

Features

Real BPM analysis

The tempo is measured from the actual audio with Essentia, a signal-analysis library used in music research. It is not read from tags or metadata.

Tap the tempo yourself

You can also tap it out by ear, with a button or a keyboard shortcut, and the panel reads the BPM from your timing.

Key detection

Key detection tuned for electronic music: one clear result, or two candidates when the track sits between keys.

Tempo Adjust

Change a track's playback speed to match your set. The key stays locked, so speeding up or slowing down won't shift the pitch, and the sound stays high quality either way.

Waveform seeking

A real, rendered three-band waveform of the track. Click anywhere on it to jump to that point.

Wishlist that keeps up

Heart albums and tracks from the panel, synced against Bandcamp's own collection data, so state is never stale.

Engineering

How it's engineered

Most player extensions borrow Bandcamp's built-in player and bolt features on top. Bandcamp Deck runs its own audio engine, built from scratch inside the browser.

Tempo without pitch shift

Tempo Adjust changes speed without touching pitch, the way professional DJ software works. The cheap shortcut that makes vocals sound like chipmunks is never used, not even as a fallback.

Gapless, like a DJ deck

Two complete players run silently in parallel. The next track loads on the quiet one while you listen to the current one, then they swap, with no gap and no click at the join.

Instant seeking

The full track is held in memory the moment it loads, so dragging the waveform is instant. No waiting for the audio to catch up, no spinner.

Analysis on your machine

BPM and key are measured from the actual audio, inside your browser, using the same library that music researchers use. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Wishlist and Collection, properly in sync

When you heart something from the panel, the extension talks directly to Bandcamp's collection API, the same one your Bandcamp profile uses. Your likes and wishlist reflect your real collection, not a guess from the page.

Set up the way you like

Open the panel's settings to make it yours: switch key analysis, auto-play, and track preloading on or off, remap the keyboard shortcuts, and tune the glass look of the panel. On Chrome there's also a performance mode that puts a powerful machine's spare headroom to work.

Built on

Two open-source libraries

The audio engine leans on two free, open-source projects from the audio world. Both run entirely inside your browser.

Signalsmith Stretch ↗

A time-stretching library. It's the part that lets a track play faster or slower without its pitch, and so its key, moving along with it. That's what keeps a sped-up track sounding natural instead of like a chipmunk.

Essentia ↗

An audio-analysis library built for music research at Pompeu Fabra University in Barcelona. It listens to the actual audio to work out a track's tempo and musical key, rather than trusting whatever tags happen to be attached to the file.
